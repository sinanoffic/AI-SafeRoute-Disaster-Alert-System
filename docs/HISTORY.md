# MCFRI Prototype History & Versioning

The Multi-Catchment Flood Risk Index (MCFRI) has evolved through multiple internal development milestones before formalization. 

## Internal Development Phases
- **V2 / V3 / V4:** Earlier development files, reports, and code comments often use internal labels such as "V2", "V3", or "V4". These labels represented incremental prototyping iterations of the deterministic risk equation, UI changes, and routing logic improvements.
- **Historical Claims:** Early internal reports (e.g. `MCFRI_Report.html`, `AI_SafeRoute_V4_Report.txt`) documented prototype demonstrations against selected historical events like the Kerala 2018 floods. These reports are preserved in `docs/archive/` for historical context. They do not constitute formal, peer-reviewed scientific validation and should not be interpreted as authoritative accuracy estimates.

## Archival Research Release
To resolve versioning ambiguity for public distribution, this repository consolidates the entire historical prototype lineage under a single canonical identity:

**MCFRI Legacy v1.0.0**

This version encapsulates the final state of the AI SafeRoute demonstration platform before the initiation of a completely separate, rigorous scientific calibration and validation programme. All future research-grade developments of MCFRI are maintained independently from this legacy repository.
